title: Policies and support
description:
  The course-wide rules for late work, extensions, academic integrity and
  getting help.
---

## Late work

Work submitted after its due time is marked down 5% of the available weight
per day late, up to five days, after which it is not accepted without an
extension. This applies uniformly across the three assessments; there is no
separate grace period.

## Extensions

Extensions are granted on request, made before the deadline, for a specific
new date rather than an open-ended delay. Ask the assessment's teacher
(named on the assessment page) rather than the convenor, unless the request
concerns the capstone, which the convenor sets.

## Academic integrity

Declare any assistance — a person, a tool, a source consulted beyond the
course's own readings — on the assessment it was used for. Declared
assistance is not itself a penalty; the reports and reviews in this course
ask for your judgement applied to supplied or chosen material, which
declared assistance does not replace.

## Content boundary

This course teaches side channels at the level of mechanism, historical
disclosure, and defensive cost. It does not teach or assess operational
exploitation of a system you do not own or have explicit authorisation to
test, and no assessment in this course asks you to produce one. Submitted
work that goes beyond that boundary will be referred, not marked.

## Getting help

For a specific week's Bench or lecture, ask that week's teacher (named on
the [people page](/people/)). For anything about the course as a whole —
extensions, the capstone brief, a concern about the content boundary above —
email the convenor.
