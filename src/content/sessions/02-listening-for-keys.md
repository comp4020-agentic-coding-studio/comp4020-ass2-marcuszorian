## Before the Bench

Record yourself typing a short, known passage on a keyboard — phone audio is
enough. Keep the passage text; you will need to check your recording against
it later.

## In the Bench

Pull the recording into a waveform viewer and align it, keystroke by
keystroke, against the passage you typed. Some keys are visibly distinct by
ear alone — the spacebar, the largest keys, anything near the recorder. The
exercise is not to build a classifier; it is to see, directly, that a
microphone captures more than it was pointed at.

## Afterwards

Bring your annotated waveform to next week's lecture, which puts a name and a
disclosure history to what you just did by hand.
