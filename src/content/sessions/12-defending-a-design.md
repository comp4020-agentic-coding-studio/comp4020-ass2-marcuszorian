## Before the Bench

Bring a draft of your capstone's device, adversary, and channel ranking —
rough is expected; this session exists to make it less rough before the
deadline, not to check finished work.

## In the Bench

Pair up and present your draft to each other in the form the capstone
actually asks for: a device, an adversary, and an ordered list of which
channels from the semester apply and in what priority. Take the critique
seriously enough to change something before you leave — a ranking that
survives contact with one sceptical peer is in noticeably better shape than
one that has only survived contact with its own author.

## Afterwards

The capstone design review is due at the end of the teaching period. This is
the last scheduled time the course gives you to find a gap in it before a
marker does.
