## Before the Bench

Bring a device you can plausibly instrument later in the semester: a laptop,
a phone, a smart card, a microcontroller — anything with a chip in it that you
have physical access to. You are not measuring anything yet. You are choosing
a subject.

## In the Bench

A short exercise: for the device you brought, list every physical quantity it
changes while it runs — current draw, temperature, the sound of its fan or
its capacitors, the light from its screen or its LEDs, the time it takes to
finish a task. None of this list is exotic; all of it is usually treated as
waste. The Bench's only job is to stop treating it as waste and start
treating it as a channel with a sender, whether or not the sender meant to
send anything.

## Afterwards

Keep the list. Each week attaches a channel from it to a mechanism, a
measurement, and a historical disclosure. By week 12 the list is the outline
of the semester, not a warm-up exercise.
