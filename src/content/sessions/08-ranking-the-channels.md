## Before the Bench

Bring your notes from every prior Bench: the acoustic recording, the power
trace, the timing plot, the shielding costing, the cache classification, the
reflection photographs.

## In the Bench

Score each of the six channels covered so far against three axes: equipment
cost to exploit, physical proximity the attacker needs, and cost to close for
a defender. None of this week's material is new measurement — it is the
first time the semester asks you to compare rather than accumulate, which is
a different and harder skill than any single week's technique.

## Afterwards

The Channel Report is due this week. It asks for the same kind of comparative
judgement, applied in depth to one channel of your choosing rather than
spread across all six.
