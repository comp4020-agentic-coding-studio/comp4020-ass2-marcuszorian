## Before the Bench

No equipment to bring — the supplied photograph set does the capturing for
you this week, so the session is about the reconstruction, not the shot.

## In the Bench

Working from a set of photographs of a monitor reflected in glasses, a
teapot, and a plain wall, attempt to recover the displayed text from each,
starting with the easiest surface. Note where recovery becomes a matter of
computational sharpening and where it becomes physically impossible no matter
how much processing is applied — a curved reflective surface and a diffuse
matte one fail for different reasons, and the difference matters more than
the failure itself.

## Afterwards

Keep your notes on which surface failed and why. This channel is the hardest
of the seven to score on week 10's rubric — the equipment is a camera and the
defence is a floor plan — and an argument you can already make about why
"hard to exploit" and "hard to defend" come apart here is worth more there
than a number you cannot justify.
