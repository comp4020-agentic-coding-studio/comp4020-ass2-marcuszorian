## Before the Bench

No recording equipment needed this week — bring the willingness to do a
costing exercise instead of a capture.

## In the Bench

Starting from a stated attenuation target in decibels, work out what shielding
a single room to that target actually requires: conductive mesh or foil
coverage, seams and penetrations (power lines, ventilation, doors), and where
the easy 80% of the attenuation ends and the expensive last 20% begins. The
number that matters is not the total cost, which varies with the room; it is
the shape of the cost curve, because that shape is why TEMPEST shielding is a
military and diplomatic practice and not a consumer one.

## Afterwards

Compare your bill of materials with another pair's. The rooms will differ;
the shape of where the cost concentrates should not.
